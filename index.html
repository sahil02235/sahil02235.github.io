27 September 2026 · arXiv:2609.33075

QureRadEmbed: Structuring Radiological Similarity through Attribute and Reasoning Supervision

Janhavi Prabhu*, Sahil*, Shivam Ashok Shukla, Manoj Tadepalli. *Co-first authors.

arXivPDFFull text

We adapt Qwen3-Embedding-4B with RadSim’s deterministic clinical-attribute ranking targets and RadThought’s hierarchical evidence supervision. Three training stages combine report alignment, single-attribute contrasts, and cross-attribute calibration. The objective is to retain distinctions in location, laterality, severity, and certainty within reusable report embeddings.

24 September 2026 · arXiv:2609.29156

Med-AR: Autoregressive Vision-Language Pretraining for Long-Tailed Chest X-Ray Classification and Uncertainty-Aware Evaluation

Janhavi Prabhu, Sahil, Akshay V, Shivam Shukla, Manoj Tadepalli, Preetham Putha.

arXivPDFFull text

We pretrain 8B and 2B VLMs with structured reports, abnormality-focused text, and region annotations. Their InternViT encoders transfer to a shared ML-Decoder architecture for long-tailed classification. Evaluation measures both discrimination and uncertainty ranking.

My contribution: model training, dataset preparation, label extraction, and manuscript writing.
