Research Publications Experience Education

I am Director of Research at Qure AI. My research focuses on vision-language models (VLMs), reinforcement learning, and multimodal reasoning for medical imaging. I work on CT and X-ray interpretation, including radiology report generation and disease-specific detection.

My work spans data curation, pretraining, supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement-learning-based post-training, clinical validation, and deployment. Earlier research includes inverse reinforcement learning, generative modeling, robust graph neural networks, and uncertainty estimation in medical image segmentation.

Research

Medical vision-language models

I develop VLMs for radiology auto-reporting and detection of pulmonary hypertension, COPD, lung cancer, and heart failure. This includes training on more than 10 million X-rays, with Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL3, Florence-VL, and LLaVA backbones, using MS-Swift and custom multimodal data pipelines.

Reinforcement learning and post-training

I work on offline and online post-training pipelines using DPO, GRPO, MPO, and GSPO to improve clinical reasoning, disease identification, and structured report generation. Distributed training work includes pipelines built with verl and NVIDIA NeMo-RL.

Clinical evaluation and deployment

My work includes multicenter validation against spirometry and specialist review, and deployment of pulmonary hypertension and COPD models at Merck and CIPLA sites. I also developed qMSK, a system for fracture detection and classification deployed at Massachusetts General Hospital and Erasmus University.

Prior research

  • Inverse reinforcement learning: learning from human feedback, with a focus on convergence and explainability.
  • Robustness and uncertainty: graph neural networks with capsule networks and Bayesian methods; uncertainty estimation in medical segmentation using PhiSeg, Stochastic Segmentation Networks, and the Probabilistic U-Net.
  • Generative vision: GAN-based image synthesis, face frontalization, multi-view generation, and super-resolution using deep unfolding and vector-quantized models.
